Research

My research examines inequality and social mobility, connecting education, labour markets, migration and economic development.

Research themesWorking papersPodcasts and interviewsPolicy and commentary

Research themes

Social mobility and inequality

How strongly do family circumstances shape life chances? I study the measurement, causes and economic consequences of intergenerational mobility and unequal opportunities.

Education and human capital

I examine how education and social policies influence skills, opportunity and development, including the long-term and intergenerational effects of economic shocks.

Migration and integration

My work explores migrant selection, the opportunities migration creates for the next generation, and policies that support the integration of migrant and refugee families.

Globalisation and technological change

I investigate how trade and new technologies reshape labour markets, change the role of family background and affect mobility across generations.

Current working papers

CEDLAS Working Paper 365 · 2026

Child Labor and the Persistence of Inequality: Evidence from the World’s Least Mobile Country

Matías Ciaschi, Mario Negre and Guido Neidhöfer

Using data from Mozambique, we examine how parental education, household wealth and children’s work relate to persistent inequality. The results point to the importance of labour market structures and the opportunity costs of schooling.

ZEW Discussion Paper 25-022 · 2025

The Long-Run Effects of Conditional Cash Transfers: The Case of Bolsa Familia in Brazil

Luis Laguinge, Leonardo Gasparini and Guido Neidhöfer

We investigate whether Bolsa Família improves beneficiaries’ outcomes beyond childhood. The analysis finds evidence of higher educational attainment and labour income, alongside a lower likelihood of receiving the transfer as an adult.

Working paper · Draft available on request

Intergenerational Returns to Migration: Evidence from Italian Migrants Worldwide

Chiara Malavasi and Guido Neidhöfer

Using administrative information on Italians abroad, we study the effects of parental migration on descendants’ education, employment and income. We examine differences across destinations and the role of opportunities for children in migration decisions.

ZEW Discussion Paper 23-042 · 2023

Trade Shocks and Social Mobility: The Intergenerational Effect of Import Competition in Brazil

Andrés César, Matías Ciaschi, Guillermo Falcone and Guido Neidhöfer

We study whether increased import competition affects the next generation. Children whose fathers were more exposed to Chinese imports have worse educational and labour market outcomes, with particularly strong effects among disadvantaged families.
